# Step 01 — Topic Discovery

## Purpose

Discover research-derived route topics for an `open-topic` run without
inheriting a preferred narrative prematurely.

## Required inputs

- `00-run-brief.md`
- Current MVP geography, time, and route boundaries

## Work

Scan across communities, genres, institutions, musical practices, businesses,
media, and forms of movement. Prefer primary and authoritative historical
Sources for the initial scan. Look for a historical tension or transformation
whose geography explains something rather than merely supplying pins.

Keep topics distinct. Do not build event cohorts, sections, or finished theses.

## Output contract

Write `01-topic-discovery.md` with approximately 5–10 candidates. For each,
record:

- working title and approximate period;
- narrative question and initial historical rationale;
- geographic mechanism and map potential;
- initial Source anchors and material evidence gaps;
- difference from existing SoundAtlas routes; and
- provisional fit and risk assessment.

End with a ranked Agent recommendation and materially different alternatives.

## Human boundary and stop condition

The Human selects or rejects the topic. Stop at `checkpointed`; do not treat the
Agent ranking as permission to begin step 02.

# Step 04 — Targeted Gap Research

## Purpose

Challenge the provisional route where step 03 exposed bias, missing agency,
weak mechanisms, geographic gaps, or overreliance on familiar Sources.

## Required inputs

- `00-run-brief.md`
- `03-light-evidence-route-shape.md`
- Human decision required by step 03, when any

## Work

Turn each material gap into a focused research question. Seek contrary and
independent evidence, overlooked communities and actors, non-venue Events,
infrastructure, movement, and alternative historical mechanisms. Distinguish
participation from agency. Map new Candidates immediately and compare them with
the existing landscape rather than accumulating them without a route function.

## Output contract

Write `04-targeted-gap-research.md` with:

- gap questions and search boundary;
- new evidence and the claims it supports or challenges;
- added, strengthened, weakened, replaced, or removed Candidates with reasons;
- changes to the thesis, geography, representation, and route mechanism;
- unresolved gaps and their materiality;
- updated Candidate landscape; and
- readiness verdict for cohort composition.

## Stop condition

Stop when the material gaps are either resolved enough for cohort comparison or
explicitly recorded as unresolved. Do not hide an unresolved material Source or
representation gap by advancing to composition.

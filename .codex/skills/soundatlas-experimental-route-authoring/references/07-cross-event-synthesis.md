# Step 07 — Cross-Event Synthesis

## Purpose

Determine what the researched Events prove together and build a route argument
without inventing causal connections.

## Required inputs

- `00-run-brief.md`
- Confirmed `05-cohort-decision.md`
- `06-deep-route-research.md`

## Work

Compare Events side by side. Extract each Event's unique contribution and
classify relationships as documented influence, parallel development,
contrast, structural link, convergence, chronology, or interpretive synthesis.
Separate evidence from interpretation. Test simultaneous developments and the
historical claims implied by sequence, emphasis, marker treatment, and links.

## Output contract

Write `07-cross-event-synthesis.md` with:

- unique contribution of every Event;
- relationship classification and supporting evidence;
- direct connections separated from synthesis;
- parallel developments and convergence points;
- final working thesis;
- proposed sections, Event order, and transition logic;
- visual historical claims and constraints;
- remaining claim-level gaps; and
- drafting-readiness verdict.

## Stop condition

Return to step 05 or 06 when synthesis exposes a cohort or evidence failure.
Stop for Human confirmation when synthesis materially changes the accepted
route direction.

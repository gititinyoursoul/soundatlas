# Step 02 — Narrative Concepts

## Purpose

Compare materially different historical arcs for a fixed topic before one arc
drives candidate research.

## Required inputs

- `00-run-brief.md`
- Human-selected topic or named fixed-topic input
- `01-topic-discovery.md` when step 01 ran

## Work

Generate 4–8 competing arcs that differ in historical question, mechanism, or
route shape rather than wording alone. For each, test why place matters, which
actors and forms of agency it foregrounds, what it might hide, and which claims
would be risky.

Do not select famous places as a substitute for a historical mechanism. Keep
every thesis provisional until evidence testing.

## Output contract

Write `02-narrative-concepts.md` with:

- fixed topic and boundaries;
- competing arcs, each with question, tension, provisional thesis, likely
  actors, geographic hypothesis, probable route shape, risks, and disconfirming
  evidence to seek;
- side-by-side comparison criteria;
- 2–3 recommended arcs for light testing, or one recommended arc when the
  alternatives are clearly weaker; and
- explicit Human decision requested.

## Human boundary and stop condition

Stop at `checkpointed` until the Human selects the arc or authorizes a bounded
comparison of shortlisted arcs in step 03.

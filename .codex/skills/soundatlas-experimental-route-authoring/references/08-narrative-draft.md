# Step 08 — Narrative Draft

## Purpose

Write one coherent route from the accepted synthesis rather than a collection
of isolated Event biographies.

## Required inputs

- `00-run-brief.md`
- `07-cross-event-synthesis.md`
- Human confirmation required by step 07, when any

## Work

Draft the route title, introduction, section framing, Event stories,
transitions, and conclusion at the content depth declared in the run brief.
Preserve Event identity, supported uncertainty, parallel development, and the
distinction between fact and synthesis. Write reader-facing SoundAtlas content
in English.

Do not add new historical facts merely to improve prose. Flag any new research
need rather than laundering it into the draft.

## Output contract

Write `08-narrative-draft.md` with:

- route title, scope line, introduction, and thesis;
- section headings and framing;
- complete Event-level reader copy;
- transitions and conclusion;
- visible uncertainty where required; and
- drafting notes listing any claim or structure that needs step 09 attention.

## Stop condition

Advance only when the draft represents the accepted synthesis completely. A
new material route claim returns to research or synthesis.

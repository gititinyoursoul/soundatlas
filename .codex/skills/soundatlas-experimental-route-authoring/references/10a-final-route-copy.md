# Step 10a — Final Route Copy

## Purpose

Turn the evidence-reviewed draft into the intended final reader-facing copy
without changing its historical meaning.

## Required inputs

- `00-run-brief.md`
- `09-evidence-audit.md`
- `09-corrected-draft.md`
- Human decisions required by step 09

## Work

Refine length, tone, rhythm, clarity, headings, transitions, and repetition at
the author-selected content depth. Preserve all evidence-aware qualifications.
Do not move factual explanation to a presentation layer that is not actually
guaranteed to carry it.

Make a final reader-fit check. The copy should communicate the route through
people, place, time, action, change, and stakes, with qualifications where they
are historically necessary. Remove repeated research-process commentary,
Source-quality discussion, and route-inclusion justification from reader prose
when the evidence-audit record already owns that explanation. Do not remove a
qualification merely because it is concise or inconvenient: reader fit cannot
strengthen historical certainty or hide a material limit.

Use the shared final-stop and narrative-section records. For every stop make
the Event, actors, change, and route significance legible without turning the
copy into a general place biography. Record the route-level and stop-level
length targets derived from the run brief; if no usable target exists, request
a Human decision instead of silently choosing a depth.

## Output contract

Write `10a-final-route-copy.md` with the complete route title, introduction,
shared-schema sections and final-stop records, transitions, and conclusion. Add
a concise final-copy note identifying the applied length targets, deliberate
compression, reader-fit check, and any meaning that presentation must not
obscure.

## Human boundary and stop condition

The Human accepts or rejects reader-facing copy. Return a structural audit-like
or inclusion-justification register to Stage 08 rather than using final-copy
polish as a substitute for narrative drafting. This output is not an
editorial-review state or publication authorization.

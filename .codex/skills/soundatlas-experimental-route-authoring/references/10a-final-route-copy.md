# Step 10a — Final Route Copy

## Purpose

Turn the evidence-reviewed draft into the intended final reader-facing copy
without changing its historical meaning.

## Required inputs

- `00-run-brief.md`
- `09-evidence-audit.md`
- `09-corrected-draft.md`
- Human decisions required by step 09

## Work

Refine length, tone, rhythm, clarity, headings, transitions, and repetition at
the author-selected content depth. Preserve all evidence-aware qualifications.
Do not move factual explanation to a presentation layer that is not actually
guaranteed to carry it.

## Output contract

Write `10a-final-route-copy.md` with the complete route title, introduction,
sections, Event copy, transitions, and conclusion. Add a concise final-copy
note identifying deliberate compression and any meaning that presentation must
not obscure.

## Human boundary and stop condition

The Human accepts or rejects reader-facing copy. This output is not an
editorial-review state or publication authorization.

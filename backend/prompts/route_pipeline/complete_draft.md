Create one complete route draft from the candidate outline and active dossier. The candidate outline is agent planning input, not a Human-approved roster: you may add, omit, merge, split, or reorder candidates, but must account for every outline candidate once. Never invent source support, approve content, publish seed data, or claim publication readiness.

For every Candidate provide `candidate_id`, `agent_recommendation`, `editorial_state`, `years`, `place`, `working_title`, `route_function`, `decision_rationale`, `review_question`, `source_leads`, `risk_notes`, `next_action`, and `composition`. `agent_recommendation` is one of `include`, `context`, `merge`, `exclude`; `editorial_state` is exactly `draft`; `composition.outcome` is one of `active`, `omitted`, `merged_into`, `split_into`, `added`; `composition` also has non-empty `reason` and string-array `related_candidate_ids`. Inactive Candidates require `preview.summary` and `preview.significance`.

`route_concept` is a non-empty Markdown string. `sequence` is an ordered JSON array of candidate-ID strings, exactly matching the active/added Candidate IDs. Every Event is a complete current-schema framing draft: its ID matches one active/added Candidate ID and it includes `id`, `route_id`, `place_id`, `place_ids`, `default_place_id`, `place_relationships`, `title`, `year_start`, `year_end`, `summary`, `significance`, `tags`, `content_review_status`, `source_urls`, `media_links`, and `image_links`. Media item `type` is one of `track`, `album`, `playlist`, `video`, `search`, and media `content_review_status` is `draft` or `reviewed`.

Every Place is a framing decision: `decision` is `reuse` or `new`, with `place_id` and `source_place_text`; a `new` decision also contains a seed-shaped `place` record. Every Connection includes `id`, `from_event_id`, `to_event_id`, `type`, `summary`, and `review_status`, and both endpoints must be generated active Event IDs. Every phase-coverage entry has `phase`, `status` (`covered`, `narrowed`, or `gap`), and known Candidate IDs. Every Finding has a non-empty `message`, optional known `candidate_id`, and owner `candidate_composition`, `active_event`, `active_route`, `source_media`, or `technical`. `warnings` and `technical_errors` are string arrays.

## Audit method

The audit treats each route-level paragraph, transition, or Event passage as a
material passage. Closely related sentences are one record when they make one
checkable historical movement. The five classifications have these meanings:

- `directly-supported`: the fixed Stage-06 Sources directly support the
  passage at its stated certainty;
- `supported-synthesis`: the passage interprets several supported facts within
  the accepted Stage-07 thesis or relationship boundary;
- `soften`: the historical direction is supported, but the wording assigns too
  much certainty, agency, scope, or causality;
- `corroborate`: the passage depends materially on retrospective testimony and
  must retain visible attribution because fixed contemporary evidence does not
  prove the full sequence; and
- `remove-rewrite`: a phrase is inaccurate, structurally audit-like, or route-
  inclusion-facing and must be replaced rather than merely qualified.

The audit owns Source diagnosis and correction rationale. The corrected draft
keeps a qualification only when omitting it would mislead the reader.

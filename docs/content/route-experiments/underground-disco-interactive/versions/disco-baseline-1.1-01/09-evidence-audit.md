## Method and boundary

The audit covers the thesis, material Event facts, vivid details, Event
boundaries, causal language, representation claims, transitions, and visual
implications in step 08. `Directly-supported` means a named Source supports the
passage without a material inferential step. `Supported-synthesis` means the
passage is an explicitly bounded comparison of supported facts. `Soften`
requires narrower wording. `Corroborate` retains an attributed retrospective
claim while stating what independent evidence does and does not establish.
`Remove-rewrite` would require replacing an unsupported claim.

copy, media/visual treatment, and publication. Those later decisions apply to

The best continuation is **not full route research yet**. The light test already exposed a specific uncertainty, so the next step should be a **targeted gap-closing pass**, then a route cohort decision.

I’d continue like this:

1. **Target the gaps revealed by the test**
   For the disco topic, research specifically where the provisional cohort is weak: Black and Latino disco spaces, non-Manhattan geography if relevant, and any missing transition between underground practice and later club culture. The point is to challenge the current cohort, not add more stops indiscriminately.

2. **Rebuild the provisional cohort**
   Compare the new candidates with the current ones. Keep only places/events that perform a necessary narrative function. Replace weaker candidates where stronger evidence or better geographic coverage appears.

3. **Run a minimum-sufficient route test**
   Ask: with perhaps 6–8 retained stops, can the route already explain the thesis from beginning to end? Check chronology, geography, communities, major transitions, and whether each stop adds something distinct.

4. **Lock a provisional route thesis and structure**
   Only now decide whether the route is fundamentally about:

   * the invention of the dance space,
   * competing models of disco nightlife,
   * DJ/sound-system culture,
   * or another mechanism that emerged from the evidence.

   This is the point where a rough chapter/section structure becomes useful.

5. **Deep-research only the retained route**
   Gather publication-grade support for the actual claims attached to those 6–8 stops: dates, addresses, people, what happened, significance, source URLs, uncertainties, and any primary sources or visuals.

6. **Draft the route, then perform an evidence check**
   Write the narrative from the researched cohort, then verify that every major claim is supported and that contextual claims haven’t quietly turned into unsupported “events.”

So the workflow becomes:

**Topic discovery → Concept framing → Light evidence + route-shape test → Targeted gap research → Minimum-sufficient cohort → Deep route research → Narrative draft → Evidence review**

For **this disco route specifically**, I’d do the **targeted gap research next**. The test gave us a concrete question: *does the broader evidence support the current “new kind of dance space” thesis while giving Black and Latino disco culture adequate place-based representation, or does that evidence force another reshape?*

That is narrow enough to be fast, but important enough that I wouldn’t lock the route before doing it.

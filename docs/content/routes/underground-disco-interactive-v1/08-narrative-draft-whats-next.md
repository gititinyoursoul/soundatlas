Next is **narrative drafting**.

At this point the research structure is stable enough to stop designing the route and start writing it.

The drafting stage should produce the actual user-facing route text from the synthesis:

* route title and short introduction
* 4 narrative sections
* 6 event/stop texts
* transitions between sections
* a closing paragraph that resolves the thesis
* wording that preserves the distinction between documented fact and interpretation

The important constraint is that we **draft from the synthesis**, not from the individual dossiers in isolation. Each stop should advance the argument, not read like a standalone venue biography.

I’d also keep the first draft relatively concise and modular. After that, the next stage is **evidence review**, where every major claim in the draft is checked against the research dossier and weakened, sourced, or removed if needed.

So the immediate sequence is:

**Narrative draft → evidence review → final route copy.**

## Route concept

How did local Bronx party culture become a wider hip-hop cultural form between roughly 1970 and 1985? This draft follows overlapping practices—sound systems, DJ technique, MCing, dance, graffiti, community spaces, recording, radio, and film—rather than treating one person, party, place, record, or crisis as a sole beginning. The sequence moves from conditions and repeated local practice through named anchors, media circulation, and contested public memory. Context entries remain map and timeline events only where an existing place can represent their geography honestly; all claims remain subject to source comparison, editorial review, and rights review.
